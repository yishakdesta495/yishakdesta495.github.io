About

I am interested in the places where law meets institutions, markets, and public policy.

My academic training is in law, but my professional development has deliberately crossed disciplines. Legal practice taught me how rules operate in real files and institutions. Research taught me how to frame problems and work through evidence. Editorial work taught me to make complex ideas readable. International mooting strengthened my advocacy skills and ability to work under pressure. Data analytics added another way to interrogate problems.

I am especially interested in legal research, governance and institutional work, public international law, international trade and economic law, and development-oriented work.
